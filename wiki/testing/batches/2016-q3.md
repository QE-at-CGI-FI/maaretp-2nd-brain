# 2016 Q3

1121 posts total, 775 after dropping retweets, 2016-07-01 – 2016-09-30
(Twitter only). The year's heaviest quarter for conflict, and a real
career turning point.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]] escalates to a public
  incident**: at Test Master's Academy (Sept 27) he presents a "How I
  differ from Maaret" slide during his own talk and won't yield the
  floor when challenged. New history surfaces too — at least one more
  block/unblock cycle beyond the documented 2014 block, and confirmation
  he publicly called her "no longer a tester" at the time of that block.
- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture]] becomes a live fight, not just
  commentary**: a painful mid-July exchange with @noahsussman and
  @AGareev over public vs. private responses to bad community behavior —
  she resists public confrontation and names the pursuit itself as
  sometimes more costly than the original offense.
- **A real job change**: leaves her employer of ~4.5 years — named for
  the first time as **Granlund** — and starts at
  **[[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]** (Sept 12), a company she'd
  worked at once before, pre-archive.
- **[[../concepts/tester-identity]]**: the Bach TMAcad slide turns the
  identity argument into a staged, public event for the first time.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: a sustained public dispute with
  @paul_gerrard over an all-male conference lineup; a formal diversity
  charter signed.
- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak]]**: she names her own exposure to the
  problem directly (paying her own way to #Agile2016) and starts asking
  for submission work itself to be paid, not just speaking.
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing]]**: the Mob Programming Guidebook
  passes 380 readers; #testgems webinar series launches; Q2's "finally
  keynoting" resolves at TMAcad — the same event as the Bach incident.
