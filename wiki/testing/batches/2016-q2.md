# 2016 Q2

1183 posts total, 817 after dropping retweets, 2016-04-01 – 2016-06-30
(Twitter only). The densest quarter yet, dominated by two intertwined
threads: a sharpening identity fight with James Bach, and the first
direct naming of a bullying/argument-culture problem in the CDT
community at large.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]] disputes whether she qualifies
  as a tester at all** (June 27) — the sharpest identity confrontation
  since the 2014 rupture. See [[../concepts/tester-identity]].
- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture|A new throughline]]**: she names
  bullying, "drone" behavior, and win-seeking argument culture directly
  for the first time, crediting both Bach and
  [[../entities/michael-bolton|Bolton]] as correctives to it while also
  criticizing their followers — and confirms, for the first time, that
  the 2014 block genuinely hurt.
- **[[../concepts/checking-vs-testing]] reignites** a third distinct time
  with Bolton ("checking is on my icky list"), then merges with the
  tester-identity fight via a week-long "what if we stopped talking about
  role" thread.
- **[[../entities/international-speaking]]**: a submission break (Apr 1)
  — but not a travel break; she still attends MobProgConf Boston,
  DevoxxUK, Testaus16, and confirms two #Agile2016 tracks plus her first
  ever keynote.
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing]]**: the Mob Programming Guidebook
  passes 250 readers; MobProgConf Boston partially answers the
  "non-programmers in mobbing" isolation question; a new ApprovalTests
  fascination begins.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: mobbing named as a possible gateway
  to #WomenInTech; a stark local contrast between an all-volunteer,
  one-woman program and a majority-female lineup getting praised reviews.
- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak]]**: real evidence the model works (45%
  women speakers + higher ticket sales, per a blog comment), plus a new
  travel scholarship.
