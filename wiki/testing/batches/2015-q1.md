# 2015 Q1

491 posts, 2015-01-01 – 2015-03-31. First quarterly batch (2015 is split
into quarters — see `../../progress.md`). The quarter her speaking career
goes international at real scale.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/international-speaking|International speaking explodes]]**:
  DEWT5, Agile Open Northwest, STPCon (San Diego), TestBash (Brighton),
  with XP2015, ScanAgile, Agile2015, and BTDconf already confirmed for
  later in the year. Self-funded, not sponsored by an employer or agency.
- **Becomes a SpeakEasy mentor**, helping new (often underrepresented)
  speakers develop proposals. See [[../concepts/gender-in-tech]].
- **Tester identity sharpens into a vocabulary experiment**: introduces
  herself as "exploratory tester" rather than plain "tester" to get past
  reflexive "you're not a real tester" reactions; pushes back publicly on
  organizations quietly renaming testers to business analysts. See
  [[../concepts/tester-identity]].
- **Possible thaw with [[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]**: no
  acknowledgment of the 2014 block, but she resumes @-mentioning him
  directly alongside [[../entities/michael-bolton|Bolton]] and engaging
  with his public writing ("Exploratory Testing 3.0"). Unconfirmed either
  way — watch future batches.
- [[../entities/teaching-kids-programming|Teaching Kids Programming]]
  scales further — Turku sessions, her own 6-year-olds group, a new
  #koodikummi volunteer program.
- [[../entities/testausyhdistys|Testausyhdistys]] expands geographically
  with a Hämeenlinna dojo.
- Work: "it's slowly starting to sink in: I work only with one product
  now" — some restructuring at her job; a strong annual review ("enabled
  my team to work much better").
