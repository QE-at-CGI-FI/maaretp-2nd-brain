# 2017 Q3

687 posts total, 450 after dropping retweets, 2017-07-01 – 2017-09-30
(Twitter only). No new conflicts; the quarter's energy goes almost
entirely into EuroTestConf 2018 and an unusually candid run of
self-disclosure about the cost of the speaking circuit.

  practice, not just selection overhead.

  publisher interest** — see [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]].

  a keynote, an early stutter she forced herself past, and a new,
  complicated admission — public speaking has materially raised her
  salary even as she campaigns against the system that made it
  exclusive. See [[../concepts/gender-in-tech]] and
  [[../concepts/pay-to-speak]].

  attribute. Embracing #awesome."

  it here" — now a hub for her local community organizing too.
- A three-stop "World tour" (Berlin, Sofia, Anaheim) shows real speaking
  volume even with the year's aggregate cutback holding.
