1214 posts total, 2020-07-01 – 2020-09-30 (Twitter only, --skip-retweets),

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture|CDT community culture]]** /
  **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]**: the Q1 "no reason left to
  exclude them" reset doesn't hold — a late-August public exchange
  reopens the "exploratory testing" credit dispute plus a new charge (that
  she threatened a student of his), and by late September she notes he's
  told others he wishes her "go to hell."
- **[[../entities/llewellyn-falco|Llewellyn Falco]]**: the Mob
  Programming Guidebook copyright/redistribution dispute from 2019-Q4
  resurfaces, prompted by a podcast that raised removing her as 1st
  author since the 2nd author is harassing her — she rejects that and
  restates the Finnish-copyright-law basis for calling his continued
  distribution unauthorized.
- **[[../concepts/exploratory-testing|Exploratory testing]]**: the
  scattered 2019 formulations crystallize into four named core
  characteristics (learning, agency, opportunity cost, systems thinking);
  a new, earlier origin story surfaces (1997, first Finland job, Cem
  Kaner's *Testing Computer Software*); "testing without testing" is
  coined for practices that invoke testing without doing its thinking.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech|Gender in tech]]**: a rare self-directed
  turn — she names her own history of erasing women co-authors (Dorothy
  Graham) and asks the same question of her audience; a decade-old HR
  complaint about sexist communication-skills training surfaces for the
  first time.
- **[[../entities/vaisala|Vaisala]]**: the summer trainee becomes a full
  colleague finding bugs independently; hands-on hardware/embedded
  testing (SIM cards, a shower-improvised rain test, hardware unit tests)
  and a manager she credits with real career sponsorship.
