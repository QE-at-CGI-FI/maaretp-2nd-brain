# 2015 Q3

979 posts total, 677 after dropping retweets, 2015-07-01 – 2015-09-30. The
busiest quarter yet for structural advocacy, and the quarter mob
programming/pairing stop being novelties and become routine practice.

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak|#PayToSpeak]] becomes a sustained
  campaign** — new this quarter, not just this year: blog posts, a
  tracked spreadsheet of her own experiences, and repeated public callouts
  of which conferences pay speakers and which don't.
- **The Helsinki Mob Programming Conference happens** (7 Sept, Woody
  Zuill), the concrete delivery on Q2's ask. Mob testing and strong-style
  pairing spread everywhere this quarter — CITCON, #testival, #TAHelsinki
  — and become a weekly personal practice, not just a teaching tool. See
  [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]].
- **[[../entities/istqb|ISTQB]] relationship pivots from "given up" to
  bridge-building** — she reframes paying for certification as funding
  CDT work rather than a concession, and speaks at an ISTQB-affiliated
  conference calling it "bridges."
- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]: a second confrontation, then
  normalcy.** A substantive mid-August clash over "kindness" (she pushes
  back harder than in June), followed by an entirely ordinary exchange in
  late September. Same pattern as Q2: real friction, no rupture, no
  resolution either.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]** at its busiest yet: the Agile2015
  t-shirt episode, a Women Keynoting mastermind group, #TAHelsinki
  speaker-diversity tracking done in real time, and a new critique —
  "most women in tech movements diss women in testing."
- **[[../entities/teaching-kids-programming]]** moves from after-school
  club into the classroom itself (opt-out removed), and her daughter
  moves from playtesting to actual small-scale public participation
  (CITCON co-hosting).
