# 2011

602 posts, 2011-01-01 – 2011-12-31. Almost 3x the volume of 2010, and
international in a way 2010 wasn't — CAST 2011 in Seattle, an ISTQB
"detractor" episode, and a week-long public debate with James Bach and
Michael Bolton over a Finnish PhD dissertation.

## What's notable

- **International context-driven testing engagement deepens.** Attends and
  presents at [[../entities/cast-conference|CAST 2011]] in Seattle. Labeled
  an [[../entities/istqb|ISTQB]] "detractor" by a marketing firm survey —
  notable because her name is on the ISTQB foundation syllabus.
- **The Juha Itkonen dissertation debate (Nov 16–20)** is the single
  biggest event of the batch: days of sharp, substantive back-and-forth with
  [[../entities/james-bach|Bach]] and [[../entities/michael-bolton|Bolton]]
  about what exploratory testing even is, whether low-quality ET can be
  told apart from non-ET, and whether academic research undervalues
  practitioner skill. She plays translator/defender for
  [[../entities/juha-itkonen|Itkonen]] as much as participant. See
  [[../concepts/exploratory-testing]].
- **Quality Center escalates from dislike to exit plan** — "on a mission
  against" it at [[../entities/ilmarinen|Ilmarinen]], evaluating Jira as a
  replacement. See [[../entities/quality-center]].
- **Documentation skepticism becomes a documented practice**, not just a
  complaint: ~2-page test plans, explicitly "never complete." See
  [[../concepts/documentation-skepticism]].
- **The test-manager/project-manager tension resolves into an actual role
  change** — gets a new test manager in her Ilmarinen project, then within
  weeks "turns to the dark side" and becomes a project manager herself
  (temporarily — the tension doesn't fully resolve until she leaves
  Ilmarinen in 2012). See [[../concepts/test-manager-vs-project-manager]].
- **Community-building scales up**: [[../entities/fast|FAST]]'s testing
  competition launches (194 bug reports, 10 teams), recurring
  [[../entities/et-dinners|ET Dinners]] peer-conferences with @ru_altom,
  more Testing Dojos, a debating-practice idea to counter what she sees as
  excessive Finnish politeness in technical discussion.
- **End-of-year restlessness**: "decided now is a good time to strive for
  a long-time dream, working abroad in a specific type of company
  (product)." Open thread for later batches — does this happen?
- Recurring named sparring partners this year who don't yet have their own
  pages — @JariLaakso, @pekkamarjamaki, @can_test — worth entity pages if
  they keep recurring at this volume in 2012+.
