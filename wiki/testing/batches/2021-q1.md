## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/exploratory-testing|Exploratory testing]]**: names a new
  umbrella, "Contemporary Exploratory Testing"; splits testing of code
  from testing of systems; names ET-as-"stealth" management directly for
  the first time; closes with a "sprinkle on top vs. sprinkle all around"
  metaphor and a "grounded management" self-description.

  the practice launched 2018 Q2 gets productized as Exploratory Testing

  Framework falling-out hardens — a concrete CI-breakage cost, an itemized
  technical grievance list, and its first reading through a gender lens
  ("men explain this to me").
- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]** /

  a 6 Feb "keynote baggage" thread retells the 2016 Q3 TMAcad incident
  with new detail (it was her own keynote debut too; Bach reportedly lost
  work over the backlash) and discloses a second, previously undocumented
  incident — an unnamed ~2017 conference organizer removed after
  harassment allegations, ahead of a keynote she gave for the same event.
