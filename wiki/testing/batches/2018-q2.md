# 2018 Q2

768 posts total, 473 after dropping retweets, 2018-04-01 – 2018-06-30
(Twitter only). The most consequential professional-identity quarter
since the 2016 Bach conflict: she becomes a manager for the first time
since 2012, and launches a new sustained teaching practice alongside it.

## What's notable

- **A career pivot**: becomes "senior manager" of two R&D teams at
  [[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]] — see the new
  [[../concepts/management-transition|Management Transition]] concept
  page for the full account (power dynamics, the timesheet-approval
  irritant, a June estimates/predictability debate tied to new
  scheduling accountability).
- **A new teaching practice launches**:
  [[../concepts/experiential-testing-training|Experiential Exploratory
  Testing Training]] — 1:1 and small-group remote sessions with
  volunteer testers, methodology worked out in public across the
  quarter (shallow vs. deep exploratory testing, "test first, ask about
  the person later").

  of collegial disagreement — "Bullying is not a business disagreement...
  James is your problem" — directly naming his enablement of
  [[../entities/james-bach|Bach]]'s behavior. See
  [[../concepts/cdt-community-culture]] for the wider theme this sits
  alongside, including her own admission of having made space for
  harmful keynote content at her own conference.
- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]]**: scouting a Valencia
  venue for the 2019 edition.
- **[[../entities/international-speaking|International speaking]]**:
  heavy spring travel (TestBash NL, StarEast, CraftConf, NTD2018,
  AgileTDUSA, SeConfIndia) followed by a real, weighed cut to the autumn
  schedule for family reasons.
- Her **Exploratory Testing Book** hits its Publish button for the
  first time.
- Smaller notes: an ApprovalTests granularity insight
  ([[../concepts/whole-team-testing]]); a "manual tester" identity
