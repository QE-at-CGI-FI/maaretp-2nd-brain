# 2017 Q4

1035 posts total, 656 after dropping retweets, 2017-10-01 – 2017-12-31
(Twitter only), the final batch of 2017. A community harassment
scandal, a personal milestone achieved, and the year's closing
accounting.

## What's notable

- **A named harassment scandal ("MrCreep")** breaks in the community —
  Lanette Creamer discloses she was a victim; an extended CoC/consent
  debate runs alongside it. See [[../concepts/cdt-community-culture]].
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: a substantial privilege exchange
  with Woody Zuill reveals real origin-story detail (she built her own
  speaking stages from nothing, 15-20 years ago); "But Women Did Not
  Submit" rejects the standard CFP excuse directly.
- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak]]**: the public tracking spreadsheet
  (maintained since 2015) draws real pushback — Romania Testing
  Conference sends "rude messages," enough to drop them from her list.
- **The keynote-speaker goal, achieved**: strong AgileTD scores close
  out the year — see [[../entities/international-speaking]]. A recount
  also complicates the Q2 "cutback held" story: 2017 wasn't actually
  fewer sessions than 2015 or 2016 (30 each year).
- **[[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]** runs a formal **#NoPO
  experiment**, removing the product owner role.
- **The 15-year-old intern** (Q2–Q3) hits a first pull request; she
  gets called "sexist" for wanting the next intern to be a girl.

## Personal content

## Year-end note

2017 closes with the keynote-speaker goal met, both books still
selling and drawing interest, a formalized workplace experiment
(#NoPO), and — after 2016's Bach-dominated conflict — a different kind
of community reckoning this year, over harassment and CoC enforcement
rather than a single relationship.
