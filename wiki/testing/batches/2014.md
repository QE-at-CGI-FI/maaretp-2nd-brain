# 2014

1540 posts, 2014-01-01 – 2014-12-31 — the biggest batch yet (nearly 3x
2010's whole year), and the most eventful. A rupture with her most
important professional relationship, a major international standards
fight, a new Finnish testing association, and a serious, ticketed push
toward relocating to the US.

## What's notable

- **James Bach blocks her on Twitter** after a role/identity debate turns
  hostile (26–27 Sept), with no explanation given. The most significant
  relational event involving him in the whole archive. See
  [[../entities/james-bach]] and [[../concepts/tester-identity]].
- **Stop ISO 29119**: she campaigns hard against the standard — Finnish
  translation work, a bruising in-person standardisation meeting at Fisma,
  and a real (though collegial) disagreement with
  [[../entities/michael-bolton|Michael Bolton]] over whether CDT should
  offer an alternative. See [[../entities/iso29119]].
- **Co-founds/deeply operationally supports
  [[../entities/testausyhdistys|Testausyhdistys]]** (Ohjelmistotestaus
  ry), a new Finnish CDT-focused testing association distinct from
  [[../entities/fast|FAST]].
- **Teaching Kids Programming reaches real scale** — multiple schools, her
  own daughter's kindergarten, Code.org's Hour of Code. See
  [[../entities/teaching-kids-programming]].
- **Gender in tech becomes a sustained professional throughline**, not
  just occasional commentary — EuroSTAR CFP advocacy, a Finnish statistics
  methodology fight, structural observations (t-shirts, "thanks guys",
  coat sizing). See [[../concepts/gender-in-tech]].
- **Reverses her own 2012 mindmap enthusiasm** — now actively dislikes
  them as default test documentation. See
  [[../concepts/documentation-skepticism]].
- **Faces a personal fear professionally**: attends a code retreat
  despite being "terrified" of pairing, blogs about it, and picks up Mob
  Programming via Woody Zuill's [[../entities/tampere-goes-agile|Tampere
  Goes Agile]] keynote. See [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]].
- Stays on [[../entities/agilefinland|AgileFinland]]'s executive committee
  (not as chair) but her footprint there barely narrows — retreat,
  micropreneurs meetups, contracts working group.
- [[../entities/testing-days-helsinki|Helsinki Testing Days]] gets a
  confirmed 2015 sequel.

## Personal content

The most substantial and most difficult personal content yet: a sister
named for the first time, her son starting school, a genuinely painful
"confessions from a bully" blog post, and a workplace incident (a sexist
comment from a colleague) she names directly and reports to her manager —
unlike 2013's more private disclosure. Most importantly: the "work abroad"
question, which the 2013 batch page called settled, turns out not to have
been — it returns this year with real specificity (Silicon Valley →
Southern California) and a booked flight. See
`../../personal/batches/2014.md` and
`../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md`.
