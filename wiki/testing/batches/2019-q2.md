# 2019 Q2

873 posts total, 2019-04-01 – 2019-06-30 (Twitter only, --skip-retweets),
second 2019 batch.

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture|CDT community culture]]**: old
  scars over James Bach reopen indirectly — Noah Sussman and Dan Ashby
  argue "exploratory testing" should be retired because Bach
  "deprecated" it; she names the pattern of men invoking authority to
  correct her, calls the pile-on itself bullying, and posts a public
  "SLA announcement" formalizing a boundary against unsolicited debate.
- **[[../concepts/exploratory-testing|Exploratory testing]]**: coins
  **#toad** (Testing, Observability and DevOps) as a defensive umbrella
  term; reframes asserts as exploration tools, not checkpoints; starts
  preparing to mark the practice turning 35 in 2020.
- **[[../entities/tech-excellence|#TechExcellence]]**: the first
  standalone edition runs 3 June in Helsinki as planned, closing 2019
  Q1's open thread — a sociology-of-tech keynote (Bourdieu) is a new
  register for the series.
- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]]**: exact honorarium
  numbers made public; real, unresolved doubt voiced about whether the
  profit-sharing model is worth its bureaucratic and tax-complexity cost.
- **[[../concepts/management-transition|Management transition]]**:
  "minimum viable management"; the 2019-Q1 self-limiting goal sharpens
  into a named one-year exit plan toward developer work; a real low
  point after leaked bugs, admitting a personal preference for testing
  over managing.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech|Gender in tech]]**: busiest hiring-
  pipeline quarter yet (30 contacts in an hour, three summer trainees,
  MimmitKoodaa sessions); a scolded female trainee; SpeakEasy's pipeline
  gets its first hard numbers (19 graduates, 120 in the mentor queue).
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing|Whole-team testing]]**: "Testing !=
  Testing," a three-filters model of tester value, and PR-review
  patterns as a diagnostic practice.
- **[[../concepts/documentation-skepticism|Documentation skepticism]]**:
  Jira tickets named directly as "a bad proxy for quality," backed by a
  three-year track record.
- **[[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]**: an accidentally polyglot team
  (C++/C#/Python/NodeJS/Go/Lua) and a telemetry-driven security insight
  from the automatic-patching work.
