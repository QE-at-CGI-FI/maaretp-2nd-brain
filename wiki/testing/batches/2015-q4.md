# 2015 Q4

725 posts total, 419 after dropping retweets, 2015-10-01 – 2015-12-31. The
quarter several running threads resolve or shift: the book from Q2
surfaces as a real product, tester identity softens on "developer," and
#PayToSpeak moves from commentary into building her own conference.

## What's notable

- **The Mob Programming Guidebook**: the Q2 book project resurfaces as a
  concrete, co-authored, self-published (Leanpub) product, actively
  selling copies through the quarter. See
  [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]] and
  `../../personal/concepts/writing-a-book.md`.
- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] takes shape** — her own
  conference (Bucharest, Feb 2016), built explicitly as a
  [[../concepts/pay-to-speak|#PayToSpeak]]-free alternative, alongside a
  smaller Teaching Exploratory Testing peer conference.
- **[[../concepts/tester-identity]] softens on "developer"**: after years
  of resisting the label, "I'm slowly accepting being a developer, I'm
  still a developer with testing emphasis. I'm different" — genuinely new
  ground, not a repeat of the identity-not-role argument.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: the Women Keynoting mastermind
  group launches for real, the all-women Halloween Game Hack surfaces
  subtler dynamics even in women-only spaces, and a DJ-ing course idea
  for girls extends the same instinct into a new domain.
- **International speaking closes the year at 33 sessions** and, for the
  first time, she states an explicit intent to slow down in 2016. See
  [[../entities/international-speaking]].
- James Bach: an ordinary, conflict-free exchange this quarter — the
  Q2–Q3 friction pattern settles into something unremarkable.
