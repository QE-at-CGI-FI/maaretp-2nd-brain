# 2013

945 posts, 2013-01-01 – 2013-12-31. The year her community organizing
outgrows testing specifically and becomes a general fixture of the Finnish
software community — plus the sharpest, most sustained technical
disagreement with James Bach in the whole archive.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/testing-days-helsinki|Helsinki Testing Days]] actually
  happens** (4 June, ~450 people) — the conference seeded in 2012 delivers,
  and its success leads directly to a second, bigger venture.
- **Joins [[../entities/agilefinland|AgileFinland]]'s executive committee**
  and launches **[[../entities/tampere-goes-agile|Tampere Goes Agile]]**
  (14 Dec, 140 people, 5 tracks) — her organizing scope expands from
  testing-specific ([[../entities/fast|FAST]]) to general agile community.
- **The year's sharpest disagreement**: pushes back at length against
  [[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]'s "Testing and Checking Refined,"
  defending her own "testing = checked + explored" formula as more useful
  than a checking-vs-testing binary. Sustained, friendly, and unresolved.
  See [[../concepts/checking-vs-testing]].
- **Becomes a commercial BBST instructor** alongside Cem Kaner, via
  [[../entities/altom|Altom]]'s new partnership with him — a real shift
  from volunteer community work toward paid, sustainable teaching. See
  [[../entities/ast-bbst]].
- **A messier ISTQB relationship**: discovers she has unrelinquished rights
  to syllabus content she co-authored, floats "FiSTS" as a Finnish
  alternative brand, still attends ISTQB events directly rather than only
  critiquing from outside. See [[../entities/istqb]].
- **Whole-team testing becomes the dominant workplace theme** — patient,
  real, sometimes emotionally costly work getting developers to test. New
  concept page: [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]].
- International visibility grows further: serves on the **EuroSTAR 2013
  program committee**. See [[../entities/eurostar]] (new page, backfilled
  with 2012 context too).
- Itkonen and Mäntylä's ESEJ paper "Are Test Cases Needed?" is accepted —
  a direct continuation of the exploratory-testing research line from 2011.
