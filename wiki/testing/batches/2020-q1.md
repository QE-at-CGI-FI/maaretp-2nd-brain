# 2020 Q1

842 posts total, 2020-01-01 – 2020-03-31 (Twitter only, --skip-retweets),
first 2020 batch. 2020 is split into quarters given its density
(4487 posts total for the year, per `../../progress.md`).

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/management-transition|Management transition]]** and
  **[[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]**: the multi-year arc resolves.
  She steps down from management internally in early February, a trust
  break with her boss follows within weeks, and by late February she's
  announced an external job change — **[[../entities/vaisala|Vaisala]]**,
  a new entity — with her F-Secure last day set for 24 April 2020.
- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]]**: the 5th edition runs
  in Amsterdam (6–7 Feb) as announced in advance to be the **last** —
  five years by design, not by attrition, with the whole organizing
  team thanked individually.
- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture|CDT community culture]]**: the
  Q4-2019 reversal on James Bach holds under a real test — she attends
  a joint Bach/Bolton session, finds nothing that reopens old wounds,
  and reaffirms forgiveness without qualification.
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing|Whole-team testing]]**: she starts
  retiring the word "Mob" itself, surveying non-men communities for
  alternatives and landing on Group/Ensemble Programming, with a plan
  to rename the Mob Programming Guidebook.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech|Gender in tech]]**: an extended thread
  drawing a direct line between a young tester near quitting and her
  own career history ("the young woman isn't me... it's like I'm
  looking at the mirror of my past"); a sharpened quotas argument; a new
  attribution-erasure instance, this time from other women.
- COVID-19 enters the record mid-quarter: conferences postponed or
  cancelled (Porto/NEXTA, ICST), and F-Secure moves to remote work in
  mid-March — noted here as a marker, not yet a developed throughline.
