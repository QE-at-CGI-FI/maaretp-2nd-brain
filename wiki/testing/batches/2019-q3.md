# 2019 Q3

890 posts total, 2019-07-01 – 2019-09-30 (Twitter only, --skip-retweets),
third 2019 batch.

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/management-transition|Management transition]]**: the
  2019-Q2 one-year exit plan becomes a real, agreed process with her
  boss ("I feel seen"); names the destination directly — "I'm a
  developer, not a manager"; a three-year technical project is named as
  the real precondition for leaving.
- **[[../concepts/exploratory-testing|Exploratory testing]]**: a public
  "we have not retired it, we are rediscovering it" rebuttal to the Q2
  Ashby/Sussman fight; a new vocabulary proposal, **attended/unattended**,
  to replace manual/automated; [[../entities/et19|ET19]]'s second
  edition runs at CAST19, resolving the open thread from both 2019-Q1
  and Q2.
- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture|CDT community culture]]**: an
  unrelated, low-stakes dispute reopens a decade-old "word corrector"
  wound from the checking-vs-testing years — confirmed as still live,
  not resolved.
- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]]**: three new organizers
  join; paying ticket-holders get formal, weighted input into
  programming for the first time; pronoun badges become structural, not
  opt-in; three paired talks confirmed for 2020.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech|Gender in tech]]**: Nokia's pay-equity
  correction (90% of fixes were women) as external validation; a sharper
  self-critique that hiring on demonstrated merit rather than potential
  structurally excludes women; MimmitKoodaa's autumn launch reaches
  ~4000 people; "nothing but white dudes in my reporting chain" named
  directly.
- **[[../entities/tech-excellence|#TechExcellence]]**: a dedicated Women
  edition crosses over with MimmitKoodaa; a new ScanAgile track-chair
  role extends the brand into an established conference.
