# 2016 Q4

1074 posts total, 708 after dropping retweets, 2016-10-01 – 2016-12-31
(Twitter only). The year closes with the James Bach conflict's
aftermath, then a real cooldown, and a quieter run of conference/teaching
content through November–December.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]] aftermath, then quiet**: the
  TMAcad fallout runs hot through the first week of October (a
  copyright-infringement threat, a mid-keynote heckle newly disclosed),
  then he doesn't appear in the archive again this quarter — the
  clearest cooldown of the whole 2016 arc.
- **[[../concepts/cdt-community-culture]]**: she declines a push to
  frame the Bach pattern as specifically gendered, names her actual
  spending-limit on the conflict ("I rather coach 10 new speakers with
  the investment"), and visibly redirects energy into #testgems,
  mentoring, and a new peer conference planned for March 2017.
- **[[../concepts/tester-identity]] settles**, at least for now, into a
  plain, unarguable definition: "I am a tester because I am hired as
  one."
- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak]]** closes the year with unusual
  self-awareness — a blog naming her *own* privilege relative to less-
  connected international speakers.
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: intersectionality named directly
  for the first time — gender and introversion as compounding, not
  separate, burdens ("1000 paper cuts").
- **[[../entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]**: the job change gets its first
  real cost — 10 lost commute hours a week the "they found me" framing
  from Q3 didn't account for.
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing]]**: Guidebook passes 397 readers;
  teaching-kids-programming scales to 30-kid sessions; a new peer
  conference with @Maaikees planned for Amsterdam.

## Year-end note

2016 closes with the wiki's most eventful year yet: EuroTestConf's
first full cycle completed and a second one underway, a real job change
(Granlund → F-Secure), the sharpest and most public conflict with James
Bach in the archive followed by a real cooldown, and a new named-child
milestone in the personal wiki. The US-relocation thread that ran since
2013 appears to have quietly ended without ever being explicitly closed.
