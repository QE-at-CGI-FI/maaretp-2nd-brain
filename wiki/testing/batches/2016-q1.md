# 2016 Q1

791 posts total, 467 after dropping retweets, 2016-01-01 – 2016-03-31
(Twitter only). The quarter EuroTestConf
actually happens and delivers on its #PayToSpeak-free promise, while two
"settled" reads from Q4 both turn out to have been premature: the James
Bach relationship and the intent to slow down speaking.

## What's notable

- **[[../entities/eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] runs (Bucharest, 11–12
  Feb)** and delivers honorariums to every speaker — see
  [[../concepts/pay-to-speak]]. Already planning a 2017 Helsinki edition.
  The parallel Teaching Exploratory Testing peer conference slips past
  its planned February date.
- **[[../concepts/pay-to-speak|#PayToSpeak]] provokes real pushback**: a
  sustained, respectful disagreement with Michael Bolton (Feb 22) over
  whether speaking is compensable work or marketing.
- **James Bach**: the Q4 "settled into ordinary" read was premature — a
  sharp Feb 2 exchange resurfaces real friction, this time naming pain
  directly rather than just disagreement. See [[../entities/james-bach]].
- **International speaking doesn't slow down** despite the explicit Q4
  intent to cut back — accepted onto two tracks at #Agile2016, plus
  TestBash, Booster2016, DevoxxUK. See [[../entities/international-speaking]].
- **[[../concepts/whole-team-testing|Mob Programming Guidebook]]**
  continues shipping incrementally (Part 2, 154 readers); #MobTesting
  keeps getting misread as "Mobile Testing."
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]**: SpeakEasy mentoring expands to a
  third and fourth mentee with a recurring public lean-coffee call; a
  sustained #ChangeTheRatio dispute over conference screening bias
  (@agiliaconf).
- **[[../concepts/tester-identity]]**: a brief "non-technical" scare at
  work (resolved as miscommunication) and a blog on identity vs. skill
  sets — consistent with, not a reversal of, Q4's softening on
  "developer."
