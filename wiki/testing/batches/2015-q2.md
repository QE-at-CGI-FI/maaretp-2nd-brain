# 2015 Q2

646 posts total, 407 after dropping retweets, 2015-04-01 – 2015-06-30. A
quarter defined by two intense vocabulary/identity debates with her two
longest-running interlocutors, and a new hands-on practice (mob testing)
that starts reshaping how she teaches.

## What's notable

- **[[../concepts/checking-vs-testing]] reignites hard with
  [[../entities/michael-bolton|Michael Bolton]]** — the sharpest round
  since 2013, running 9–15 June. New ground covered: whether creating a
  check is itself testing, the gatekeeper-role metaphor, and a real,
  named personal cost — "I've been seriously considering stopping work on
  software. The industry needs to learn to be nicer." Two blog posts
  process it afterward.
- **[[../entities/james-bach|James Bach]]: friction, not the thaw Q1
  hoped for.** A tense 26 June exchange has her explaining, at length,
  that his "chosen style" makes her life harder running the Finnish CDT
  non-profit, and ends self-aware rather than resolved ("I need to learn
  to stop these"). See [[../concepts/tester-identity]] for the adjacent
  identity thread with Bolton the same weeks.
- **Mob testing, first-hand**, at #xp2015 — "changes how I teach." Feeds
  into a broader push at [[../entities/agilefinland|AgileFinland]]'s Tech
  Excellence pod (TDD mobbing, legacy code workshops) and, by quarter's
  end, an ask about organizing a full mob programming conference in
  September. See [[../concepts/whole-team-testing]].
- **[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]** busier than any quarter since 2014:
  an EsConfs keynote-selection critique, a public commitment to consider
  female experts, a blog on women leaving programming, and man-woman
  pairing requests for kids' coding sessions.
- **[[../entities/teaching-kids-programming|Teaching Kids Programming]]**:
  her own kids playtest Linda Liukas's Hello Ruby with her — the project
  reaches into her own household as direct participation, not just
  inspiration.
- International speaking pace holds: STPCon, Agile San Diego, BTDconf
  (talk, workshop, and a keynote in Estonia), plus an ET course in
  Brighton and a FiSTB Testing Assembly talk both booked for later in the
  year. See [[../entities/international-speaking]].
