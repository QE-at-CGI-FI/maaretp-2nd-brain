# 2012

639 posts, 2012-01-01 – 2012-12-31. The year the multi-year
test-manager/project-manager tension actually resolves, and her community
organizing shifts from participant to institution-builder.

## What's notable

- **Leaves [[../entities/ilmarinen|Ilmarinen]]** (her employer since at
  least 2010, named directly for the first time this batch) on 27 March,
  after actively job-hunting since February — including a "long-time
  dream" application to work abroad at a large product company (see
  `../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md`).
- **Takes a hands-on tester + test automation role instead**, still in
  Finland, with a remote team — and explicitly resolves the
  [[../concepts/test-manager-vs-project-manager|test-manager/PM tension]]
  by choosing testing over management: "I feel like a tester again."
- **Documentation skepticism produces its sharpest evidence yet**: a
  47-page doc cut to 2 pages without information loss; a 39-page/46-test-case
  doc replaced by a one-page mindmap that catches 3 things the original
  missed. See [[../concepts/documentation-skepticism]].
- **Becomes FAST chairman** and hands off the testausOSY chair to Pekka
  Marjamäki. Co-founds [[../entities/laatu-ja-testaus|Laatu & Testaus]], a
  Finnish testing magazine. Starts planning
  [[../entities/testing-days-helsinki|Testing Days Helsinki]] (June 2013)
  with [[../entities/altom|Altom]]'s [[../entities/et-dinners|@ru_altom]].
  See [[../entities/fast]].
- **The ISTQB relationship gets stranger, not simpler**: discovers a
  consultant reusing ISTQB Foundation syllabus text — which she herself
  co-authored. Still pushes back on reflexive ISTQB-hate among peers. See
  [[../entities/istqb]].
- **Empirical research on testing becomes a real project**, not just an
  ambition — mining her own bug-fix data, reading qualitative-methods
  books, working with [[../entities/juha-itkonen]] toward an academic
  article. See [[../concepts/testing-research]].
- **International visibility grows**: tagged alongside Michael Bolton and
  @eviltester in the 2013 EuroSTAR CFP announcement.
- **Explicitly skips CAST 2012** — a deliberate reallocation toward
  building Finnish/European infrastructure over attending the US flagship
  event every year. See [[../entities/cast-conference]].
