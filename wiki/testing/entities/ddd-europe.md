# DDD Europe

A Domain-Driven Design conference (@ddd_eu) in Amsterdam. First appearance
in the archive: she keynotes there 1 February 2019.

## 2019 Q1 — first keynote — [[../batches/2019-q1|batch]]

Keynotes 31 Jan–1 Feb, framing it as a natural extension of her
testing-community collaboration calls rather than a new audience she
sought out: "The causal relationship of what made this happen is a little
fuzzy but I attribute it to [[eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] collaboration
calls. #DDD and #testing communities have a lot in common." Her
[[../batches/2017-q4|No Product Owner]] material lands well with this
audience — someone reframes it back to her as "Engineers love solving
problems, not implementing someone else's solutions," a phrase she adopts
afterward to explain the idea at work. Posts slides to SlideShare and
closes the trip naming the DDD community's warmth directly: "felt a lot of
sympathy of souls with folks there."
