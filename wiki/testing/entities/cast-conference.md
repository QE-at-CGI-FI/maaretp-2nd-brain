# CAST (Conference of the Association for Software Testing)

AST's annual conference. First appearance in the archive as an aspiration
(missed the CFP window, Jan 2011); becomes a real trip by August.

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Attends CAST 2011 in Seattle (8–11 Aug) and **presents** — "finally happy
with what I'm about to present," uploads slides to Slideshare afterward.
Engages actively with conference content: the session-based vs.
thread-based test management distinction becomes a point she chews on for
weeks afterward (see [[../concepts/exploratory-testing]]). Talks with a
CAST newcomer over dinner about what practicing context-driven testing
actually looks like day to day.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Explicitly skips it: "I'm skipping CAST this year. With all the work and
other places to be in, I can't do it every year. Trusting people will
blog." Not a loss of interest — she's simultaneously ramping up
[[ftpc|FTPC]], [[testing-days-helsinki]], and [[laatu-ja-testaus]] —
reads as a deliberate reallocation toward building Finnish/European
infrastructure over attending the flagship US event every year.
