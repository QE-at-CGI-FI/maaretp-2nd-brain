# FAST — Finnish Association for Software Testing

The organizational hub of her community-building work in Finland (~800
members by 2011). She's an active organizer, not just a member — runs board
matters, promotes events to the membership, and repeatedly reminds people
that mailing-list access isn't the same as membership.

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

- Runs a member survey during a session: of 45 respondents, only 5 felt
  uncomfortable with ISTQB criticism — she reads this as less resistance to
  the [[istqb]] critique than she expected.
- Invites [[michael-bolton]] to speak to FAST, defending giving ISTQB board
  members room too: "there's room for ISTQB board marketing too."
- Organizes/promotes the first [[fast-testing-competition|FAST Testing
  Competition]].
- Runs "testausOSY" (a FAST-adjacent special-interest group/mailing list) —
  redoing its web pages, planning lightning-talk and debating practice
  sessions, book circles (ordered *How to Reduce the Cost of Software
  Testing*).
- Repeated Helsinki [[weekend-testing-and-dojos|Testing Dojo]] sessions
  under FAST's umbrella; tries again to get a Tampere dojo going.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

She's now **chairman of FAST** (~1000 members) — introduces herself this
way to an outside contact wanting to partner with the Finnish community.
Hands off the testausOSY chair specifically to Pekka Marjamäki in June, and
ships a new testausOSY website in November (a side project she'd been
sitting on since a missed end-of-2011 deadline). Continues nominating for
Finnish Tester of the Year and running the nomination/voting process, with
some friction over an unlisted voting link getting shared to 950 members
anyway.
