# Testing Days Helsinki

A free, two-track testing conference in Helsinki, first planned for June
2013. Seeded in April 2012 as a vague "special kind of testing conference"
decided with [[et-dinners|@ru_altom]] over drinks; by November it has a
name, a date, and a planning lunch scheduled.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Still in planning through the whole batch — no 2013 edition covered yet.
Notable as her first venture into organizing something at conference scale
rather than dojo/dinner/competition scale, and as a joint project with
Altom ([[altom]]) rather than purely a [[fast|FAST]] initiative.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Happens: 4 June 2013, free, ~450 participants (some last-minute
cancellations from an even bigger signup), a Test Lab running concurrent
with the talk tracks. Reads as an unqualified success — "super excited,"
"community is a nice thing to have" — and validates the whole-conference
format enough that she goes on to found
[[tampere-goes-agile|Tampere Goes Agile]] the same year.

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

Confirms she's running it again, June 2015: "feeling ridiculously happy...
Helsinki Testing Days are in my doing pipeline - next June." Briefly
considers giving it up in December (schedule conflict with a US trip
opportunity) but doesn't.
