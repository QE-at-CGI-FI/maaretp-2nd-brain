# AST BBST

The Association for Software Testing's Black Box Software Testing course
series.

## 2010 — [[../batches/2010|batch]]

Takes the BBST Foundation course in Nov–Dec 2010, considers it "somewhat
fun, somewhat isolated." Explicitly frames it as an alternative to ISTQB she
wants to promote, and thinks a Finnish version would eventually be needed.
Finishes the course, dreads the grading/exam feedback but doesn't avoid it.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Enrolls in BBST Bug Advocacy (June), hoping it's less groupwork-heavy than
Foundation. Actively promotes AST as a feasible alternative to
[[istqb|ISTQB]] for tester training, floating a Cem Kaner-adapted online
version that could scale without resorting to multiple-choice exams.

Representative posts:
- https://twitter.com/maaretp/status/165863824384204800
- https://twitter.com/maaretp/status/265015197570306048

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Moves from student to **commercial instructor**: [[altom|Altom]] partners
with Cem Kaner to deliver BBST Foundations, and she's teaching a BBST
commercial course alongside Kaner in January 2014 — sold out before the
year ends. Frames the shift explicitly as sustainability, not just
credibility: paid courses let her (and Kaner) "be out of other work to
teach," unlike volunteer-only delivery. Also states she doesn't teach AST
BBST *content* herself in the AST-branded sense but is now delivering the
latest version, including a new workbook, in Finnish. In an ISST
founding-members dispute in August, explicitly says she prefers AST's
values over the new group's approach.

Representative posts:

- https://twitter.com/maaretp/status/400539921191944192

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

Teaching load grows: delivers AltomBBST classes regularly (translating
Foundations to Finnish), takes BBST Domain Testing herself as a student in
parallel — "I never have to work this much on any other courses, I like
this" — and starts 2015 co-teaching a commercial BBST course with Cem
Kaner again.
