# ISO 29119 / Stop29119

An ISO software-testing standard the context-driven testing community
organizes hard against in 2014, arguing it entrenches document-heavy,
pre-agile testing practice and risks being cited in contracts/regulation
regardless of merit.

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

She's an active campaigner, not a bystander: signs and pushes the
Stop29119 petition, translates critique
material to Finnish, and is invited to (and attends) a Finnish
standardisation meeting at Fisma to represent the opposition — a
genuinely bruising experience: "worse than I expected... like a nightmare
where I can't do anything but laugh," written up in "Lessons Learned at a
Finnish Standardisation Meeting."

Produces the year's other substantial disagreement-with-an-ally: pushes
[[michael-bolton]] on why the CDT community won't offer any alternative
standard/framework of its own, arguing a real vacuum invites 29119 to fill
it. He holds firm that declaring an alternative "standard" repeats the
same mistake; she doesn't fully concede the point. Separate from the
[[james-bach|Bach]] rupture, but the same autumn — a season of friction
with her closest professional allies, not just with the standard itself.
