# Quality Center (QC)

Test management tool used at her workplace throughout 2010. Consistently
ambivalent-to-negative about it.

## 2010 — [[../batches/2010|batch]]

- "I'm not comfortable with any of them [ways of using QC]. Used it often
  though."
- Repeatedly prefers Excel: "excel is better option, since it counts and
  colors the cells automatically and adds visual status info."
- Sees it as enabling a management fiction — used so "the One Responsible
  would be able to see all tests planned & run. He never looks though."
- Blames QC-shaped thinking for at least one batch of bad test cases
  ("Fixed already though").

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Escalates from dislike to active exit plan: "on a mission against Quality
Center. Caused so much trouble in last week that I have good chance of
getting rid of it in next six months." Investigates Jira as a replacement
for test case/log structures. Generalizes the complaint beyond QC
specifically: "all tools as only communication channels are evil."
