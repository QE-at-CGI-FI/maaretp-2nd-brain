# Tampere Goes Agile (TGA / #trega13)

A free [[agilefinland|AgileFinland]] conference she launches, first edition
14 December 2013 (a Saturday).

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

140 people across five tracks, sponsored by Solita, CGI, Gosei, Leonidas,
Metosin, Vincit, and [[altom|Altom]]. Includes a Global Day of Coderetreat
track running concurrently. Some no-shows prompt an open-letter blog post
about the real cost (~2500€ of sponsored beer/food) of unused signups.
Immediately commits to a 2014 edition despite the exhaustion: "I'm happy we
organized #trega13... committing to year 2014."

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

#trega14 runs 25 Oct with Woody Zuill keynoting on Mob Programming —
introduces a practice and a collaborator who stays relevant into 2015
(she joins his remote "skeleton team" mob-programming session on New
Year's Eve). Sponsored again by FiSTB, Affecto, CGI, Gosei, Leonidas, and
Vincit.
