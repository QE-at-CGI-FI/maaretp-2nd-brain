# FAST Testing Competition

A [[fast|FAST]]-organized bug-hunting competition, first run 28 Oct 2011
(rescheduled from an earlier date after learning Fridays/weekends don't
work well for a work-context event).

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

10 teams, colocated and distributed participation. Kickoff 16:00, deadline
20:30. Ends with 194 bug reports filed. Modeled partly on CAST's
developer's-choice award, adapted to "manager's choice" since there's no
developer track here. She spends the following days categorizing the bug
reports.
