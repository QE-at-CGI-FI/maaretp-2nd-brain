# Twitter → Mastodon Migration

## 2022 Q4 — a values-driven exit, not a slow drift — [[../batches/2022-q4|batch]]

The archive's platform transition itself becomes a documented event, not
just a silent handoff between raw sources. In late October, days after
Elon Musk's Twitter acquisition closes, she names the reason plainly
rather than treating it as inevitable: "Like everyone else preparing to
escaping twitter, I too am thinking of it," followed by requesting her
full Twitter data export "to process it elsewhere into something
useful." The actual move happens fast: by 18 November she's posting from
Mastodon directly, "going into my 2nd day without Twitter and saying my
goodbyes to friends who stay there, I am sad" — explicit grief
alongside the decision, not a clean break. The stated reason is a
values line, not a feature comparison: "Can't show up with contents on
a platform where employees are treated like they are though. I'm ok
with downsizing, even radical one, but not like this... Can't
contribute to the space and stay congruent to what I believe in in the
world." Restated more plainly weeks later: "I left when the
mistreatment of employees became a thing. I felt strongly I cannot
produce contents for a business that treats people like that." This is
the same pattern of principled, sometimes costly consistency the wiki
already tracks in `../../personal/concepts/self-understanding.md`'s
2022-Q2 "idealism" reflection. The transition isn't purely a loss: by
mid-December she reports finding "new people, and many I like better
here," aided by tools like fedifinder to relocate her existing
follow-list. On 16 December she begins archiving all 27,304 tweets via
the Wayback Machine ("feels like an end of an era"), undecided at the
time whether to delete them afterward. Her professional posting
continues uninterrupted through the transition — #TestingDozen,
#SeConf, and #ContemporaryExploratoryTesting content appear on both
platforms either side of the switch — this is a platform change, not a
pause in the record.

## 2023 — the deletion completes, and the transition holds — [[../batches/2023|batch]]

The December-2022 "undecided" thread resolves in July: "Deleting all my
contents on twitter... Deleted 2 years of twitter contents, everything
with a picture and now deactivated hoping it will delete as promised
all in 30 days. Was about time. Leaving the people behind took me quite
some time..." A real, named worry follows — that walking away would
cost her the annual Top 100 ICT-influencers-in-Finland listing she'd
made four years running — but by December the fifth consecutive listing
lands anyway: "acting on what I believe is right is still net
positive." Restated once more, plainly, why she left: "I left when the
mistreatment of employees became a thing. I felt strongly I cannot
produce contents for a business that treats people like that. Having
come here, I feel I found new people, and many I like better here."
