# Exploratory Testing Dinners

A recurring small-group peer-conference format organized mainly with
@ru_altom, distinct from [[weekend-testing-and-dojos|Testing Dojos]] —
discussion-based rather than hands-on testing.

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Multiple rounds through the year (June, Sept, Oct, Nov, Dec). Topics
include bug reporting practices, cultural differences, and Petri Kuikka's
"manual testing radiator" idea (which she blogs about). She explicitly
prefers smaller groups: "smaller group, better discussion. Or different
people, better discussion." Some rounds run under [[fast|FAST]]'s banner,
some not.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Continues (Jan, Feb) with @michaelbolton joining a January round. By
autumn the more structured [[ftpc|FTPC]] format seems to absorb most of
the organizing energy — fewer standalone "dinner" mentions this year.

Representative posts:
- https://twitter.com/maaretp/status/159715939108143104
- https://twitter.com/maaretp/status/172799715975507968

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Passes to other organizers — @pr0mille runs one in February — while her
own organizing energy shifts toward [[agilefinland|AgileFinland]]'s peer
conferences. A sign the format has become self-sustaining rather than
dependent on her.
