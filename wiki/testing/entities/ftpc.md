# FTPC — Finnish Testing Peer Conference

Formal peer-conference format, running since 2005 (originally FiWST —
Finnish Workshop on Software Testing — renamed FTPC). Distinct from the
more casual [[et-dinners|Exploratory Testing Dinners]]: structured
sessions, invited attendees, explicit format design.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Runs sessions in Helsinki, including one with [[james-bach]] in October.
She's hands-on in the format design, not just attending: talks through how
to adjust to whether an audience wants to listen, talk, or skip a session,
uses voting stickers and timeboxes to manage continuation votes, and
identifies a recurring failure mode — mixing senior and newcomer attendees
frustrates the seniors, so invitee selection is "key." One session (#FiST)
includes a strong disagreement with a newcomer over the usefulness of bug
seeding that she describes as one of the best parts of the event.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Places itself explicitly within a wider European network she tracks: "PEST,
SWET, DEWT, LEWT, FiST, RoWST — what else?" LEWT in particular is a
recurring destination — she hopes for a third attendance. FTPC/FiST itself
gets a strong-disagreement moment she calls one of the best parts of an
event: arguing bug-seeding usefulness with a newcomer at length.
