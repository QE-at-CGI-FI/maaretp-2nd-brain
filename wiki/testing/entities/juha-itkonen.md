# Juha Itkonen

Finnish computer-science researcher; PhD dissertation (defended Nov 2011,
Aalto/HUT) on real-world exploratory testing, including "The Role of
Knowledge in Failure Detection During Exploratory Software Testing"
(ITSE, Nov 2011).

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Subject of an extended, sharp but respectful multi-day debate (Nov 16–20)
between her, [[james-bach]], and [[michael-bolton]] about his dissertation
and its methodology. She's the mediator/translator role here as much as a
participant: she knows Itkonen personally, defends his research effort and
patience with academic constraints ("I respect Juha for his patience I
never had"), while agreeing with Bach/Bolton on some substantive critiques
(he "fell into the trap" of treating scripted-vs-exploratory as a clean
binary; low-quality ET is hard to distinguish from non-ET).

Her own position, distilled: "I'm too stuck with the multimeaning
exploratory testing. At least I care for better testing, ET or not." — she
uses the dissertation debate to sharpen her own thinking about what "ET" is
even a useful label for. See [[../concepts/exploratory-testing]].

She's actively interested in academic research on testing: wants to
co-author an academic article within two years, considered joining
Itkonen's research group as a company case study, and reads his papers
closely enough to critique their literature framing.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Presents on test oracles at an ESPA research seminar — "the last one in
his dissertation," closing out the work debated in Nov 2011. Agrees to
help her turn her own latest project into an academic-quality-audience
article, kicking off the [[../concepts/testing-research|empirical research]]
work she does through the rest of the year.
