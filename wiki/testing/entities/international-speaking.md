# International Speaking Career

Not a single event but a phenomenon worth tracking on its own: 2015 is the
year her conference speaking goes from occasional to a defining, expensive,
self-funded part of her professional life.

## 2015 Q1 — [[../batches/2015-q1|batch]]

Within three months: DEWT5 (Netherlands, Jan), Agile Open Northwest
(Seattle area, Feb), STPCon (San Diego, Mar–Apr), TestBash (Brighton,
Mar) — plus confirmed slots later in the year at XP2015 (Helsinki),
ScanAgile, Agile2015 (Washington DC), and BTDconf (Belgium). She names it
herself: "My year of acceptances includes #scanagile, #testbash, #xp2015,
#agile2015, #stpcon and #btdconf. Kind of cool."

The financial reality behind the glamour is explicit: "getting expensive
to travel around the world to speak. Not working for a consultancy that
would pay it for me. Still thinking." She's self-funding this circuit,
not travelling on an employer's or agency's budget — connects directly to
`../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md` and the open question of
whether to go independent, join an established consultancy, or relocate.
Also starts exploring the practical mechanics of US work: "work permit in
US is hard. Wondering what is required for European consultancies to sell
their services delivered by europeans."

Becomes a SpeakEasy mentor this quarter (see
[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]) — helping new speakers, particularly
women, get through their first proposals, paying forward what she's
learned from years of conference organizing.

## 2015 Q2 — [[../batches/2015-q2|batch]]

The pace holds. STPCon (San Diego, Apr 1–2) and an Agile San Diego
meetup talk open the quarter; a keynote and workshop follow at #BTDconf,
plus — new this quarter — "a keynote in Estonia." She announces an
**Exploratory Testing Work Course in Brighton, UK (Oct 22)** with the
Ministry of Testing, and books a talk on "Intrapreneurial Testing" for
FiSTB Testing Assembly in September. No sign of the pace slowing or the
US work-permit/consultancy question from Q1 resolving — see
`../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md`.

## 2015 Q3 — [[../batches/2015-q3|batch]]

Quantifies the pace herself: "looks like I've done 19 talks this year, 8
more to go." Highlights: a full week at Agile2015 (Washington DC,
including [[../concepts/gender-in-tech|the t-shirt episode]] and a
#WomenInAgile meetup), #testival in Croatia, #TAHelsinki (FiSTB Testing
Assembly, an ISTQB-affiliated event — see [[istqb]]), and a running
[[../concepts/pay-to-speak|#PayToSpeak]] advocacy thread throughout.
No new movement on US relocation this quarter — see
`../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md`.

## 2015 Q4 — the year closes at 33 sessions, and she calls it enough — [[../batches/2015-q4|batch]]

Final tally for the year: 33 sessions. TestBashNY and AgileTD anchor the
quarter, alongside building [[eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] as her own
conference. For the first time, she states an explicit intent to slow
down rather than keep climbing: "you make my resolution to not travel to
as many conferences next year harder," and a blog post on "speaking goals
2016 and accomplishments 2015." The US visa/relocation question — parked
all through Q3 — reactivates hard this quarter; see
`../../personal/concepts/career-direction.md`.
