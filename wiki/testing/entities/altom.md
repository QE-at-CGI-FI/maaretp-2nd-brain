# Altom

A Romania-based testing company/community founded by @altomalex and
[[et-dinners|@ru_altom]] ("Not Finnish, but with a strong Finnish
presence"). One of her closest ongoing professional collaborations outside
Finland.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

- Books a trip to Romania (rOWST — Romanian testing conference/workshop) —
  "yet another place I've never been to. New place + testers, should be
  fun."
- Publicly credits @altomalex, @ru_altom, and "Oana" for testing
  discussions she values.
- Ru Cindrea (@ru_altom) and Kristoffer Ankarberg chosen as 2012 Test Lab
  Apprentices — she RTs and celebrates this.
- Starts planning a peer conference of European peer-conference organizers
  with @ru_altom, floating Romania as the location.
- Nominates @ru_altom (after the fact, regrets the timing) for Finnish
  Tester of the Year as "the person who has made a difference in Finland
  for the testing community" despite not being Finnish.

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

Runs the Test Lab at Belgium Testing Days (@TheTestLab, with
@adinnaplus) — TDD with a Lego robot, black-box physical objects, a
"brutal refactoring game" she calls out of her comfort zone, mobile and
performance-tools sessions. Ships iTester v3.0. @coveredincloth — an Altom tester she's worked
closely with all year, praised repeatedly as brilliant — finishes her
contract on Maaret's team in December; Maaret writes a public "hire her"
post.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Ships iTester (a session-based test management tool, v2.0 adds SBTM report
