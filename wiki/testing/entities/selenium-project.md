# Selenium Project

## 2022 Q3 – 2023 — from new committee seat to sustained volunteer leadership — [[../batches/2023|batch]]

Joins the **Selenium Project Leadership Committee** in 2022 Q3 (see
`../entities/international-speaking.md`), and by 2023 this becomes one
of her most sustained volunteer commitments, running alongside — and at
times competing with — her paid management role. She helps organize
**SeleniumConf Chicago** (28-30 March 2023), visibly strained behind the
scenes by visa delays and rerouted flights for international speakers.
Later in the year she co-organizes a free online **Selenium Open Space
Conference (SOSC23)**, timed to the project's 19th anniversary (28
October 2004 launch, confirmed via original blog/archive research), and
begins planning its 20th-anniversary celebration for 2024. She frames
her own contribution modestly against paid open-source maintainers —
"my little time is way too little" — while still becoming, by year end,
the first person to put both volunteer time and personal money into the
project (a $20 Open Collective donation, "just to test it"). A recurring
theme: defending Selenium's relevance against "old-school" framing from
newer tools built on top of it, while personally preferring Playwright
day to day for its API design — not a contradiction she experiences as
uncomfortable, just a practitioner's choice layered over a maintainer's
loyalty.

## 2024 — the 20th anniversary, and a scholarship program — [[../batches/2024|batch]]

Marks two years on the Leadership Committee with a reflective blog post
and a stage slot at SeleniumConf's June online edition (334
participants). The project's 20th anniversary lands 28 October
(confirmed by her own archival research the previous year into the
exact October 2004 launch date), and she marks it with a blog post
crediting the community over the tool: "Selenium is the friends we made
along the way." She frames Selenium's real work as standardization, not
the library itself — pointing to Playwright's own move to implement
WebDriver BiDi as evidence the whole ecosystem, competitors included,
runs on protocols Selenium pioneered. Sets up a travel-scholarship
program for underrepresented groups to attend SeleniumConf Valencia,
continuing the pattern from EuroTestConf and her broader speaker-
sponsorship work.

## 2025 — three years in, and stepping back — [[../batches/2025|batch]]

In July, three years into the Leadership Committee, she weighs whether
to continue, change how she volunteers, or leave: "I am starting to be
inclined on the first [leaving]... Dice sounds like a plan?" Weeks
later, alongside stepping back from other non-profit boards and
leadership groups, the decision firms up: "I finally made a decision:
no more volunteering for leadership groups for now. The upcoming year
will be challenging in many ways, and I need my hobbies to be
recharging me, not being about difficult organizational problems" — a
direct consequence of the burnout she names the same season (see
`../concepts/management-transition.md`). She still shows up as a
practitioner and community member — organizing a Selenium demo,
proposing the project as an analysis target for a friend's open-source
AI tooling project in December — but the sustained leadership-committee
commitment tracked on this page since 2022 ends.
