# EuroSTAR (#esconfs)

Europe's largest testing conference.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

First appears as a marker of growing visibility: she's tagged alongside
[[michael-bolton]] (named 2013 Programme Chair) and @eviltester in the
2013 CFP announcement.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Serves on the **program committee** — two days of committee work leave her
newly passionate about things she "did not realize were an issue before."
Attends EuroSTAR 2013 with program-committee duties, facilitation, and
[[altom|Altom]]'s Test Lab all scheduled. Volunteers (repeating an offer
from two years earlier) to help organize a future EuroCAST.

## 2020 Q4 — an award, a Dorothy Graham moment, and a 2004 callback — [[../batches/2020-q4|batch]]

Receives EuroSTAR's award (unnamed in her tweets beyond "#Testing has
two major awards and I have now them both," alongside 2016's MIATPP),
prompting a reflective list of the causes she credits: #PayToSpeak,
#TechVoices, #EnsembleTesting/#EnsembleProgramming, #StrongStylePairing,
#ExploratoryTesting, #TestAutomation, #ModernAgile, #AwesomeTesters.
During the same conference, hearing Dorothy Graham speak in person moves
her to tears — see
`../concepts/gender-in-tech.md` for how this connects to a Q3
self-criticism about erasing women co-authors. Separately, reviewing
her own history, she finds her first-ever international talk was at
**EuroSTAR '04** ("Planning for Plan-Driven vs. Agile Testing") — a
concrete date for a milestone this wiki previously only had as "her
first international talk" without a year.

## 2021 Q1 — a program-chair "no women of merit" post, recalled — [[../batches/2021-q1|batch]]

A 6 Feb retrospective (see
[[../entities/international-speaking|International Speaking Career]] for
the full context) traces part of her years-long difficulty becoming a
keynote speaker back to a specific person: "a popular speaker, then
program committee chairman for EuroSTAR posted about 'no women of
merit'" after roughly her first decade of speaking (so, around the
early 2010s) — read by her directly as commentary on whether she
qualified. Not previously dated or attributed this specifically in the
wiki.

## 2021 Q2 — a keynote booked, and the wound dated to April 2015 — [[../batches/2021-q2|batch]]

Announced as an **EuroSTAR 2021 keynote speaker** ("Exploratory Testing
when CI/CD is a given") — explicit closure on the Q1 disclosure: "it is
also, for me, a result of hard work." A 13 May retelling corrects the
Q1 dating: the "no women of merit" episode happened in **April 2015**
(not "roughly her first decade of speaking" as estimated in Q1 — she'd
actually been speaking 14 years by then) and is now attributed to "a
group of testers in the community loosely connected with the
conference" rather than a single named program chair. See
`../../personal/concepts/self-understanding.md` and
[[../entities/international-speaking|International Speaking Career]]
for the fuller account, including two related but not fully reconciled
grievances from around the same period.

## 2021 Q4 — the keynote delivers, feedback stings, and the dating shifts again — [[../batches/2021-q4|batch]]

Thanks the conference for its student-access program right after her
keynote lands. Feedback comes back mostly strong with one anonymous
negative comment, and she names the disproportionate weight it carries
plainly: "I obsess over the ONE negative and never want to speak in
public again." A follow-up tweet shifts the "no women of merit" dating
yet again — "I became keynoter out of spite for 'no women of merit'
which was stated for EuroStar **5 years ago**" (so, ~2016, not the Q2
account's April 2015) — the wiki now has two inconsistent datings from
her own retellings four months apart and can't resolve which is right.
This tweet adds one new detail neither prior account had: "the guy I
was compared to in the comment" — implying the original "no women of
merit" remark set her against a specific named man, not stated here.
