# Testausyhdistys (Ohjelmistotestaus ry)

A new Finnish testing association ("Software Testing ry"), founded 14
April 2014 explicitly to advance context-driven testing in Finland —
distinct from [[fast|FAST]], not a replacement for it.

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

Grows fast: 69 members within weeks of founding. She's deeply operationally
involved — logo design contest, a Selenium 2/WebDriver study group,
recurring articles, presence at Tampere Testing Days (#testaus14) and the
Talentum Software QA & Testing Summit (#sqats14), and TestPro (a
recruitment/training program for unemployed people entering testing) —
though she's openly critical of TestPro's ISTQB-heavy content, unwilling
to hire from it as-is despite limited alternatives. Publishes Finnish
[[iso29119|Stop29119]] material through the association's channels ahead
of the Fisma standardisation meeting.
