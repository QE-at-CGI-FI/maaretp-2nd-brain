# ET19 (Exploratory Testing peer conference)

A LAWST-style peer conference on [[../concepts/exploratory-testing|exploratory
testing]] "after 35 years" — first previewed as an unnamed spin-off workshop
in [[../batches/2018-q3|2018 Q3]] (Valencia, 16 Feb 2019, ~20 people), then
delivered and named in [[../batches/2019-q1|2019 Q1]]. Co-led with @charrett
and **Alex Schladebeck** (@alex_schl) — the same trio behind the 2016
[[eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] TDD/ET content.

## 2019 Q1 — first edition, and a sequel planned — [[../batches/2019-q1|batch]]

Runs directly after [[eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]] closes (16 Feb,
Valencia). She frames it as rediscovery, not nostalgia: hoping to recover
exploratory testing as "the smart testing not everyone was doing at the
time," not a fixed historical technique. Session notes are collected and
published on a dedicated web page. Before the quarter ends, a second
edition is already planned — **ET19 ed2**, alongside @AST_News immediately
after [[cast-conference|CAST]] in August 2019.

## 2019 Q3 — second edition, at CAST19 — [[../batches/2019-q3|batch]]

Runs as planned: "Today is #ET19 where we figure out more of
#ExploratoryTesting after 35 years. Thank #CAST2019 for the great
extension for the conference with a peer conference" (16 Aug, Cocoa
Beach, FL). Session content stays close to the first edition's spirit —
searching for a "story coverage" mnemonic with @charrett, discussing how
DDD's bounded contexts change story-based testing, and a running bit
naming attendees' "superpowers" (synthesis, holding space, structuring,
recognizing opportunities for change). A sharp one-liner from the day
sums up the community-culture tension the series exists partly in
response to: "you need to create a wall of sociopaths that are good, to
protect from evil sociopaths."
