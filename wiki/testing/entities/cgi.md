# CGI

## 2024 — her employer from June, Director-level testing leadership — [[../batches/2024|batch]]

Her next employer after `vaisala.md`, joined 3 June 2024 as **Director**,
leading testing services with an explicit AI focus. The company
announces the hire with a press release ("Maaret Pyhäjärvi nimitetty
CGIn johtajaksi") and Finnish IT media covers it too, one headline
dubbing her "testausguru" (testing guru) — titles she finds a little
funny given her self-image as someone who "knows nothing and
experiments with everything to learn." The role is consulting/
contracting rather than product work: multiple concurrent customers,
a target of roughly 130 customer meetings a year, and building her own
team from scratch (dozens of applications reviewed per open position).
She negotiated, and got approved, the ethical groundwork for using
GitHub Copilot on production code — risk assessment, tool-version
upgrades, and three compensations: public transparency about the
experience, time invested in open source, and money donated to
sustaining open-source projects. The job also brings her back to public
speaking (see `international-speaking.md`) after her 2023 retirement,
starting with a talk the day after she joined.
