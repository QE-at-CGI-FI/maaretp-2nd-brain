# Ilmarinen

Her employer through [[../batches/2010|2010]] and [[../batches/2011|2011]] —
referred to only as "the pension insurance sector" in those batches; named
directly for the first time in 2012, on her way out the door. A large
Finnish pension insurance company (data-intensive systems, subcontractors,
customer-side acceptance testing).

## 2010 — [[../batches/2010|batch]]

Already there by the archive's earliest tweets (May 2010), pushing
[[../concepts/exploratory-testing|exploratory testing]] into a sector that
doesn't practice it, against real resistance. Ends the year with a
foothold: first acceptance test completed after 16 months in the role, and
early signs of management support for the shift from traditional to
exploratory testing.

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

The [[../concepts/test-manager-vs-project-manager|test-manager/PM tension]]
plays out here for the first time: gets a new test manager in December,
briefly returns to non-management work, then within days "turns to the
dark side" and becomes a project manager herself — a preview of the
resolution that doesn't actually land until she leaves in 2012.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

- Continues as test/project manager into Q1 2012, then actively job-hunts
  ("now actively looking for new work... moving on as soon as possible"),
  blogs about the search, and departs 27 March 2012 — parting note: "as a
  go-away present, last week's results are worth about some hundreds
  thousands," and "so excited about being TESTER again."
- The irony of the exit: a consultant at her *new* workplace reuses text
  she wrote — it turns out to be a section of the ISTQB Foundation
  Syllabus, which she co-authored. See [[istqb]].
- Ilmarinen posts her old job (Testauspäällikkö / test manager) after she
  leaves; she comments the posting has adapted the description toward what
  she was actually doing.
