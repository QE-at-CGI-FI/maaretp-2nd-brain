# #TechExcellence

A recurring Helsinki meetup on technical-agile practice (unit testing, test
doubles, refactoring) she's been facilitating since at least
[[../batches/2017-q1|2017 Q1]] (544 members that quarter — see
[[../concepts/gender-in-tech]]). In [[../batches/2019-q1|2019 Q1]] it grows
into a standalone one-day conference.

## 2019 Q1 — from meetup to conference — [[../batches/2019-q1|batch]]

Co-organizes a new single-track **"Technical Agile" conference**, launching
in Helsinki 3 June 2019, with @rinkkasatiainen and @malk_zameth — venue
booked and CFP opened within the same week. Explicitly modeled on the
profit-sharing, speaker-paying design of [[eurotestingconf|EuroTestConf]]:
"I'm organizing another conference that doesn't only pay expenses but
profit shares." The framing problem she names while pitching it to a
director-level colleague — "the word that bothered them was agile... a
better term on teaching software craft could be in order" — echoes the
same audience-naming problem the [[ddd-europe|DDD Europe]] keynote
surfaced the same quarter. The existing meetup keeps running in parallel:
a hands-on session on mock/stub/spy/fake/dummy vocabulary with
@rinkkasatiainen draws a direct callback to
[[../concepts/whole-team-testing|golden master testing]] terminology
precision.

## 2019 Q2 — the first edition runs, closing 2019 Q1's open thread — [[../batches/2019-q2|batch]]

**#TechExcellenceConf runs 3 June 2019 in Helsinki as planned**, closing
the open thread from 2019 Q1 about whether the June date holds. A packed
single-track lineup, live-tweeted session by session: @AmelieCornelis on
becoming technically excellent ("F.A.I.L = First Attempt In Learning"),
@EskoLuontola on leaky abstractions and TDD's long-run effect on
designing testable structures, @hellofatester on shared maintenance
ownership through [[../concepts/whole-team-testing|mob programming]],
@crawled_away on architecture and Conway's Law, @mavi888uy on serverless
monitoring, and @rinkkasatiainen on functional programming
("Railroad-Driven Programming"). The closing keynote, @malk_zameth on
Bourdieu's social/cultural capital applied to tech ("there is no
meritocracy but a rigged social system"), lands as a genuinely new
theoretical register for the conference series — sociology rather than
technique. Runs as one of **three conferences in two weeks** alongside
SocratesUK and TestingCup, plus two training days and a podcast/magazine
interview — the newest and heaviest conference-organizing commitment
sitting inside an already dense speaking stretch.

## 2019 Q3 — a Women edition, an architecture kata meetup, and a new track chair role — [[../batches/2019-q3|batch]]

The meetup format keeps running alongside the standalone conference: a
1.5-hour architecture kata using C4 modeling draws a full house in
Helsinki, and a dedicated **"#TechExcellence Women edition"** brings
[[../concepts/whole-team-testing|mob programming]] to a
[[gender-in-tech|MimmitKoodaa]] audience specifically — the two
initiatives (technical-practice meetup and women-in-programming
teaching) now visibly cross-pollinate rather than running as separate
tracks of her work. A new institutional foothold: **invited to chair
the #TechExcellence track at ScanAgile**, extending the brand beyond her
own self-organized events into an established conference's program
structure.
