# Vaisala

Her next employer after [[f-secure|F-Secure]] — a Finnish measurement
instruments company. Named directly for the first time in
[[../batches/2020-q1|2020 Q1]], after a job search she'd announced
publicly in 2019 Q4 resolved: "the call I have been excitedly looking
for came today: I will be changing jobs in a few months and super
excited about the new challenges ahead in a different company."

## 2020 Q1 — named, ahead of an April start — [[../batches/2020-q1|batch]]

Confirms the name in a brief reply ("Vaisala") while still working out
her notice period at F-Secure (last day 24 April 2020). Frames the move
as a genuine test of transferable skill rather than just a fresh start:
"looking forward to reapplying the skills I learned here on another
place to see if I actually learned how to do quality and productivity
transformations." No detail yet on team, role, or product — that
begins in the next batch.

## 2020 Q2 — starting, and a title without the manager label — [[../batches/2020-q2|batch]]

Starts 27 April: "First day at a new job. It reminds me a lot of the old
job and I love the puzzles of taking on new projects and products."
Her role is **principal engineer**, testing embedded software — a
genuine departure from F-Secure's Windows-application context, close to
hardware for the first time: "found some helpful colleagues and
appreciate even more how code makes things concrete over just the
black-box." Names the real contrast with the management role she left
directly: "there is significantly less administrivia for being a
principal engineer than there was for an engineering manager... I
change things by doing, in collaboration. I like this." Inherits a
Robot Framework/Selenium test automation codebase she's openly
skeptical of ("prison break... as soon as it runs, I'll delete and
replace it") while working across a JavaScript/React team. Takes on a
summer trainee in June, building a deliberate day-by-day teaching
curriculum — a continuation of the trainee-mentoring practice from
F-Secure, transplanted into the new company within her first two
months.
