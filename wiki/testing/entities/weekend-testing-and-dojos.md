# Weekend Testing & Testing Dojos

Peer-practice testing formats she engages with but doesn't fully embrace as-is.

## 2010 — [[../batches/2010|batch]]

- **Weekend Testing**: likes the idea but not the name/timing — "around here
  practicing testing skills could happen during weekdays," later repeats
  "weekends are reserved for family."
- **Testing Dojo**: attends a Helsinki dojo organized by @tkiuru, finds it
  "mostly about observing" and less active than she'd like — contrasts it
  with weekend testing's more active format. Notes the Tampere dojo will run
  as a workshop instead. Also organizes/promotes a SOA testing mini-seminar
  for the Finnish Association for Software Testing (13 Jan, Helsinki).

Reads as her actively shopping for/designing the right format for building
local, in-person testing community in Finland, rather than adopting an
existing format wholesale. See also [[../batches/2010|2010 batch]] —
"building a great testing community in Finland."

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Testing Dojo #2 runs in Helsinki (organized by @plyytinen). A paired
exploratory-testing workshop in November fills 9 of 12 seats; she admits
she's "bad at pairing, need more practice" and needs a personality match to
pair well — a rare moment of naming a personal skill gap rather than a
structural/organizational problem. See also [[fast|FAST]], which now hosts
these alongside the newer [[fast-testing-competition|Testing Competition]]
and [[et-dinners|ET Dinners]] formats.
