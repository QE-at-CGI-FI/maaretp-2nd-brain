# AgileFinland

The Finnish agile community association — broader than testing, her main
vehicle for community work beyond [[fast|FAST]] from 2013 on.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Arrives to a meeting an hour late, announces she wants to join the
executive committee without introduction, gets elected anyway. From there,
organizes at a rapid pace: monthly-ish Agile Breakfast sessions (Finnish
and English), peer conferences/unconferences on Scaling and No Estimates,
a coding-dojo "roadshow," a Future of Agile (Finland) fishbowl discussion
livestreamed on Google Hangouts, and — the big one —
[[tampere-goes-agile|Tampere Goes Agile]]. Explicitly wants to bring
testing-community-style peer conferences to a non-tester audience: "I want
to try testing community style peer conferences with non-testers."

## 2014 — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

Stays on the executive committee (not as chair — Jussi Markula is elected
chairperson at the June annual meeting) but her footprint barely narrows:
runs the #afretreat strategy weekend, launches an "Agile Micropreneurs"
(self-employed) meetup series, a contracts-focused working group, and —
the biggest new thread — builds
[[teaching-kids-programming|Teaching Kids Programming]] into a real
multi-school program. Also absorbs most of her
[[fast|FAST]]-era organizing energy this year: more AgileFinland breakfast
events, coaching circles, and peer conferences than testing-specific ones.
