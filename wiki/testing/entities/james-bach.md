# James Bach

Context-driven testing figure (@jamesmarcusbach). One of her two main
interlocutors in 2010 for working out the [[../concepts/checking-vs-testing]]
vocabulary — long reply threads on negative/non-confirmatory testing and
"demonstration magic."

## 2010 — [[../batches/2010|batch]]

First appearance in the archive. Exchanges are substantive, not just
mentions — she pushes back on his framing as often as she agrees.

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Central figure in the Nov 16–20 marathon debate about [[juha-itkonen]]'s
dissertation — dozens of replies over five days on what exploratory testing
even means, whether low-quality ET is distinguishable from non-ET, and
whether academic research undervalues it. She defends Itkonen against
Bach's frustration while agreeing with several of his specific points.
RTs his "FDA endorses exploratory testing of medical devices" and shares
his "A Tester's Commitments" post as something that "belongs on every
tester's wall." Also references "ET dynamics" material by "the Bach
brothers" as a source for paired-testing ideas.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Visits Finland multiple times to teach: open courses in June, then a
public RST class and "Critical Thinking for Software Testers" in October,
plus the two-day **Rapid Testing Intensive (RTI)** she attends and credits
with teaching her "a few things about myself" — a rare admission from her
that a course changed her self-understanding, not just her technique. She
also notes wryly that ISTQB-heavy Finnish companies seem to avoid his
courses — "bad timing or high portion of ISTQB certified people." Asks him
and [[michael-bolton]] to write a proper reference article defining
exploratory testing that incorporates recent RST material, something
closer to the "dynamics paper" than a blog post.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

The year's sharpest disagreement with him. After his "Testing and Checking
Refined" post, she pushes back at length (Feb): his RST students, she
argues, often leave with a diluted "versus" message rather than the
nuance, and her own "check + explore" framing (see
[[../concepts/checking-vs-testing]]) does the same job without the
adversarial framing. He pushes back that softening the message loses
force; she holds her position — "the stronger the words, the less they
listen" — while being explicit she isn't calling anyone a liar and respects
his stance. A rare case of sustained, friendly disagreement with him that
doesn't resolve either way. Still recommends his work constantly (TAD, RTI
follow-ups, students) and teaches with his materials.

## 2014 — rupture — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

The relationship breaks. A long Twitter exchange (26–27 Sept) about
whether "role" is a useful concept — she's arguing for identity over
assigned role, see [[../concepts/tester-identity]] — escalates until he
says he's upset with her. She tries to de-escalate and steps away to
"shop groceries." The next day she discovers he's blocked her, with no
explanation given then or later. She calls it "really bad habits" and
seriously considers distancing herself from the CDT community over it,
before deciding the community is bigger than one person. Nothing in the
rest of the batch suggests the block is lifted or the relationship
repaired. Earlier in the year, before the rupture, relations were warm:
she defends his approach in a debate with @Morendil, and their working
relationship (courses, references, recommendations) continued normally
through September.

## 2015 Q1 — cautious re-engagement? — [[../batches/2015-q1|batch]]

No acknowledgment of the block anywhere in this quarter, but she resumes
publicly discussing and directly @-mentioning both him and
[[michael-bolton|Bolton]] together (e.g. asking them jointly why they
chose "checking" over renaming testing itself), and responds to their
"Exploratory Testing 3.0" post with her own "not ready for it yet" blog.
Whether the block itself was lifted isn't confirmed either way — see
[[../concepts/tester-identity]].

## 2015 Q2 — friction, not a thaw — [[../batches/2015-q2|batch]]

The cautious re-engagement from Q1 doesn't hold. A tense exchange on 26
June (in the context of a wider CDT/Australian testers spat) has her
saying she's spent "a lot of time explaining CDT != you," that his
"chosen style" makes her life harder, and — in a line that reads as her
quoting back something said to her rather than her own voice — "Grow up,
Maaret. Stop expecting you care. Finland is fine." She ends the thread
self-aware rather than resolved: "I need to learn to stop these. Let me
just say I respect your professional contribution a lot + you as a person
I've met," and separately, "I will never stop believing, but I will stop
making an effort." No mention of the 2014 block itself this quarter, in
either direction — but the tone answers the Q1 "possible thaw?" question:
not a rupture repeat, but not warm either.

## 2015 Q3 — a second confrontation, then normalcy — [[../batches/2015-q3|batch]]

A different, more substantive clash in mid-August, this time explicitly
about the word "kind": he tells her some of what she says is unkind; she
pushes back harder than in June rather than de-escalating — "kindness
would be to then listen to me," and "you might have a track record of
treating good people bad and thus making misjudgements. Your truth isn't
universal." Also defends the CDT people around her against his charge
that they're shallow ("if you consider them shallow, you haven't found
the right topic... to go deeper"). By late September the relationship
reads as ordinary again — an unremarkable exchange about Finnish sauna
and drinking culture, no residual tension visible. Consistent picture
across Q2–Q3: real, recurring friction that neither ruptures further nor
resolves.
