# Laatu & Testaus

A Finnish-language testing magazine ("Quality & Testing") she co-founds in
2012, three issues a year, theme-based (first theme: Past, Present and
Future).

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

Forms the board (three people), collects articles for issue 1 without
knowing yet what they contain, and publishes it by 31 Aug — "finally
Laatu & Testaus 1/2012 is out." Writes one article herself, describes the
groupwork across timezones as the hard part, not the content. Explicitly
frames this as vacation-time "community work," not paid work.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

Issue 2/2013 ships (Dec, credited mainly to @Matti_Vuori's push). Theme for
an upcoming issue: "controversies in testing" — she notes wryly that
"'small minority' is against certification," a comment on how one-sided
the Finnish testing community's own views can be even on a page meant to
showcase controversy. Gets a strong article this year: an interview with
someone with 50 combined decades of testing experience (she jokes it's "5
different decades," not a rounding error).
