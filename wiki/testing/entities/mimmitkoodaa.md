# MimmitKoodaa

A Finnish women's-coding initiative ("mimmit koodaa" — women code), aimed
mostly at adult career-changers rather than kids — a parallel effort in
spirit to [[teaching-kids-programming|Teaching Kids Programming]] but
for a different age group. She starts teaching for it in autumn 2018.

## 2018 Q4 — launch of her involvement — [[../batches/2018-q4|batch]]

Joins the spring-season launch (2 Oct): 1500 women signed up across
Finland, 800 training days on offer. Teaches a Java pairing session at
[[f-secure|F-Secure]]'s own premises alongside other F-Secure women, and
recruits open tester positions directly through the community. By
December the group has grown to "3000 women wanting to learn this
stuff." By mid-November, though, she redirects her volunteer teaching
time away from the outward-facing MimmitKoodaa sessions specifically
toward women colleagues inside her own company instead — "I have so
many ladies inside the company wishing for a bit of my teaching time
that I'll choose to focus there" — suggesting the external commitment
takes a back seat to internal mentoring by year's end.
