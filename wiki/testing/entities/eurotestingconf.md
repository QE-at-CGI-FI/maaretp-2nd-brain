# EuroTestConf (European Testing Conference)

A conference she's centrally organizing (@EuroTestingConf), first floated
in [[../batches/2015-q3|Q3]] and built out heavily in
[[../batches/2015-q4|Q4]], aimed at Bucharest, February 2016. Explicitly
designed as a living counter-example to [[../concepts/pay-to-speak]] —
"don't #PayToSpeak, join #EuroTestConf as speaker."

## 2015 Q3 — [[../batches/2015-q3|batch]]

First appears as one of the conferences she names as *not* requiring
speakers to pay — grouped with #TestBash and #AgileTD in her running
#PayToSpeak commentary.

## 2015 Q4 — [[../batches/2015-q4|batch]]

Becomes a major throughline: crafting a questionnaire for early-bird
ticket buyers on desired content, building a session-selection "labels"
mindmap with contributor input, choosing between competing submitted
talks, and reaching out to [[../concepts/gender-in-tech|SpeakEasy]]
mentors for newer speaker topics. She frames organizing it as one of the
best parts of her year — "one of the best things about organizing a
conference is to get to talk to many amazing people sharing." A parallel,
smaller initiative grows alongside it: a **Teaching Exploratory Testing
peer conference**, also planned for Feb 2016, aimed at people who teach
public ET courses, possibly aligning with Let's Test / Copenhagen
Context's entry criteria.

## 2016 Q1 — it runs, and it delivers on the promise — [[../batches/2016-q1|batch]]

Happens as planned (Bucharest, 11–12 Feb). Live-tweets it heavily — Linda
Rising's growth-mindset keynote, Alex Schladebeck on the two schools of
TDD, sketchnoting, presentation-karaoke sessions — and closes by
patiently waiting for the last invoices so she can calculate "the
honorariums _every_ speaker gets. Speaking is work," the concrete proof
of the [[../concepts/pay-to-speak|#PayToSpeak]]-free design working, not
just being promised. Already planning a 2017 edition in Helsinki
("see you all in Helsinki Finland in a year!") and considering an online
"Lift Pitch" video format for it. The parallel **Teaching Exploratory
Testing peer conference**, planned for the same February window, doesn't
appear to have happened on schedule — a Feb 1 post is still "looking for
a better time" for it, key people unavailable — open thread for whether
it happens later in the year.
