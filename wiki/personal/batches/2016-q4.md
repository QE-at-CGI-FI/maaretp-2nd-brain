# 2016 Q4

1074 posts total this quarter (708 after dropping retweets), Twitter
only, 2016-10-01 – 2016-12-31.

- **[[../entities/family]]**: the biggest single personal-wiki event of
  the year — her **daughter is named for the first time: Emilia, 7
  years old** — crediting her artwork in a conference slide, then two
  warmer follow-ups (Python vs. Blockly, a "fixed mindset" comment
  landing as a small parenting win). The son stays unnamed.
- **[[../concepts/self-understanding]]**: the introvert/extrovert
  contradiction, live since 2011, gets its most concrete treatment
  yet — a TestBash talk on introversion prompts a blog post ("Ruining
  the life of an introvert") and a new observation that gender and
  introversion compound rather than run separately ("1000 paper cuts").
- **[[../concepts/career-direction]]**: the F-Secure move's first real
  cost surfaces — 10 lost commute hours a week the Q3 "they found me"
  framing didn't anticipate. No further movement on the (likely
  abandoned) US-relocation thread.

## Year-end note

2016 is the wiki's most eventful year yet on the personal side too: a
