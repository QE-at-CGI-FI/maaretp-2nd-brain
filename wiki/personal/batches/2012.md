# 2012

639 posts total this year, still overwhelmingly professional, but personal
content grows from a handful of posts (2011) to a real if minority
thread — and this year it's entangled with a major professional decision
rather than separate from it.

- A **brother** appears for the first time — she calls him to re-orient
  when she loses her own sense of place. See [[../entities/family]].
- **Kids start kindergarten**; the "two kids" note from 2011 sharpens into
  a real time/capacity tension: "I no longer own my non-work time." See
  [[../entities/family]].
- **Career direction**: the 2011 "work abroad" dream gets acted on (an
  actual application in Feb) and then quietly redirected — she leaves her
  job but takes a Finland-based hands-on role instead, and by year's end
  reframes the whole year as being about *role* (tester vs. manager) more
  than geography. See [[../concepts/career-direction]].
- A rare admission of a genuinely bad day ("I just feel like becoming a
  gardener instead... I kill flowers... feeling desperate today") and an
  unexplained but real claim that a course "taught her something about
  herself." See [[../concepts/self-understanding]].

Read together with the testing wiki's `../../testing/batches/2012.md`,
this is the year the professional throughline (test manager vs. tester)
and a personal throughline (who she wants to be, where) resolve at the
same time, via the same decision.
