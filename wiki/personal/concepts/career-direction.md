# Career Direction

## 2011 — [[../batches/2011|batch]]

Named as a "long-time dream" at year's end: working abroad, at a large
product company. First appearance of career direction as something she
frames in personal, not just professional, terms.

## 2012 — [[../batches/2012|batch]]

The dream gets acted on and then quietly redirected:

- Feb 2012: "dared to take the step for a long-time idea and sent my
  application to positions abroad in a large product company."
- Within days, two *domestic* job discussions are also open, and she says
  she'd "prefer if the choice could be quick" — the abroad application
  doesn't seem to have become the obvious front-runner even to her.
- Leaves Ilmarinen in March; the job she actually takes is not abroad — a
  hands-on tester/automation role, still in Finland, with a remote team.
- By October, looking back at the whole year: "I've moved up to be a
  better tester. Needed to sidetrack in management though, but now back."
  The frame has shifted from *geography* (abroad vs. Finland) to *role*
  (management vs. hands-on testing) as the axis that actually mattered.

## 2013 — [[../batches/2013|batch]]

No mention of working abroad this year at all — the question from 2012
reads as settled. Her direction instead broadens *locally*: elected to
[[../../testing/entities/agilefinland|AgileFinland]]'s executive committee,
launches [[../../testing/entities/tampere-goes-agile|Tampere Goes Agile]],
becomes a commercial BBST instructor alongside Cem Kaner. The "abroad"
dream seems to have been replaced by a different kind of expansion — wider
scope and more institution-building within Finland, rather than relocation.

## 2014 — correction: it wasn't settled — [[../batches/2014|batch]]

2013's silence turns out to have been a pause, not a resolution. The dream
comes back this year with more force and more specificity than in 2012:

- Feb: her son's school placement decided ("need to let my son get
  started with school"), but frames it as a 1–2 year runway to relocating
  — location "USA & silicon valley," specifically.
- March: "a week in conference and I'm thinking when would be an
  appropriate time for my kids to move out of Finland. Hard balancing
  call" — the question has shifted from *whether* to *when*, and now
  centers the kids' timeline as much as her own.
- Summer: a parallel, half-serious startup/independence track opens up —
  "quitting dayjob for a startup," seriously weighing established
  consultancy vs. founding her own — before she seems to set it aside.
- Aug–Nov: "today is the day for a decision" (Aug 15) passes without a
  visible resolution in the tweets; by mid-November the US relocation
  reopens explicitly — "Relocating to US - back to this project" — with
  one job application already rejected since April, and the target
  narrowed from Silicon Valley generally to "strong preference to southern
  California."
- Nov 26: books flights Helsinki–San Diego–Amsterdam–Helsinki, combining
  a US conference (STPCon, San Diego, spring 2015) with DEWT5 in the
  Netherlands — the first concrete, ticketed step toward the US in the
  archive.
- Year ends, though, with signals pulling the other way: a new manager
  starting January 2015 opens by giving her an unprompted raise, leaving
  her "feeling very confused" rather than settled on leaving.

Net effect: the 2013 note calling this "settled" was wrong, or at best
described a lull. The honest state at end of 2014 is unresolved — real
momentum toward the US (a booked trip, a specific target region) sitting
alongside a job that just got more attractive. Watch 2015 closely for
which way this breaks.

## 2015 Q1 — [[../batches/2015-q1|batch]]

The US thread stays warm without resolving: regular travel to San Diego
(STPCon) and elsewhere, and a concrete practical question — "work permit
in US is hard. Wondering what is required for European consultancies to
sell their services delivered by europeans" — suggests she's now
exploring *how* rather than *whether*. At the same time she names the
real cost of the international-speaking path she's on instead: "getting
expensive to travel around the world to speak. Not working for a
consultancy that would pay it for me." See
`../../testing/entities/international-speaking.md`. No resolution yet —
relocation, consultancy, and staying employed in Finland are all still
live options simultaneously.

## 2015 Q2 — parked, not progressed — [[../batches/2015-q2|batch]]

No new movement on relocation or the work-permit/consultancy question
this quarter — the thread stays exactly where Q1 left it, warm but
unresolved. The international-speaking pace itself keeps climbing
instead (STPCon, Agile San Diego, BTDconf including an Estonia keynote,
plus a Brighton course and a FiSTB talk booked for later in the year) —

## 2015 Q3 — no movement — [[../batches/2015-q3|batch]]

No US-relocation content at all this quarter — not even the practical

## 2015 Q4 — correction: it reactivates hard — [[../batches/2015-q4|batch]]

The "no movement" read from Q3 turns out to be another lull, not a
settling — same pattern as the 2013→2014 correction. At #TestBashNY:
"need to remind again for people... that I'm looking for the perfect
#testing job in US, needing visa sponsor. Long process," and "would
prefer west coast but quite open on location for the right job." The
international-speaking pace closes the year at 33 sessions with an
explicit stated intent to slow down in 2016 — see
`../../testing/entities/international-speaking.md` — which sits in
tension with an active US job search: if the goal is to relocate, does
slowing the speaking circuit help or hurt that? Not addressed in the
tweets themselves.

## 2016 Q1 — from search to logistics — [[../batches/2016-q1|batch]]

The Q4 reactivation moves a step further, from job-searching language
into a concrete relocation detail: "in prepping to relocate to another
country, I should get rid of my physical books. Pack up and share here?"
(Feb 8) — treating the move as something to plan around practically, not
just a stated intention. No country, timeline, or employer specified.
Sits in continued tension with
`../../testing/entities/international-speaking.md`'s Q1 finding that the
speaking pace, rather than slowing as she'd said in Q4, actually grew —
two threads pulling in opposite directions (settle down to relocate vs.
keep expanding the speaking circuit) without either resolving.

## 2016 Q2 — a domestic job decision, and a second, unusual interview — [[../batches/2016-q2|batch]]

No further movement on the US/relocation thread specifically this
quarter — this batch's job activity reads as ordinary domestic
job-market activity rather than a continuation of it. Mid-April: a real,
weighed decision — "Are making decisions about potentially changing your
job supposed to be easy? Where is my clarity?" followed four days later
by "I ended up choosing Quality of life and family over cool stuff to do
professionally. That was a hard decision" — she turns down or steps back
from something professionally appealing in favor of family/quality of
life, without naming what it was. A different, later interview (May 27)
reads as more collaborative than a rejection: "I was in a different kind
of job interview yesterday. It felt figuring out what job to build for
me. Hiring for roles can be replaced." See
`../../testing/entities/international-speaking.md` for the same
quarter's submission break, a related but separate pullback.

## 2016 Q3 — a real job change, and the relocation thread appears to quietly die — [[../batches/2016-q3|batch]]

Two developments, one resolved and one likely abandoned:

- **The job move resolves**: leaves her employer of ~4.5 years
  (unnamed until now — Granlund) on Sept 2, starts at
  **[[../../testing/entities/f-secure|F-Secure]]** on Sept 12, a company
  she'd worked at once before, pre-archive. Framed as passive, not
  sought — "they made me feel they want me... I wasn't really looking" —
  a different register than Q2's effortful "chose quality of life and
  family" decision; the wiki can't confirm whether these describe the
  same opportunity or two separate ones.
- **The US/relocation thread, likely dead**: early in the quarter (July
  2) she says "Preparing to change countries. So this is just
  practical" while giving away belongings — consistent with the Q1
  book-packing logistics. But by September she's taken a new job at a
  Finnish company, describing it as "home ground." Nothing in the
  archive explicitly closes the relocation question, but taking root in
  a new Finnish role reads as the thread quietly ending rather than
  resuming — flagged here as likely abandoned rather than asserted as
  confirmed, since no tweet directly says so.
